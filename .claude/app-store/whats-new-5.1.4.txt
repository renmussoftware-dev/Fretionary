What's new in Fretionary 5.1

TRIADS INSIDE ANY SCALE
• See the major and minor triads that live inside the scale you're playing. Tap a triad to hold it on the neck and hear it.
• Label triads by chord role (R / 3 / 5) and narrow them to a 3-string set. Root position, 1st and 2nd inversions are outlined and color-coded.

3-NOTES-PER-STRING PATTERNS
• A new Position style toggle: Standard or 3 per string.
• 3NPS works for every scale, including pentatonic, blues and whole tone, with each string's span highlighted as it climbs the neck.

BETTER SCALE BOXES
• Pentatonic and blues boxes are now the true two-notes-per-string shapes, with the blue note inside each box.
• Positions are numbered the standard way. Box 1 is the shape with the root on the low E, in every key.

CAGED, REBUILT
• CAGED shapes now show the full scale window for each shape and repeat all the way up the neck.
• Major and minor CAGED, with Scale, Pentatonic, Arpeggio and Chord layers.
• Custom progressions: reorder chords, then save, update or delete them by name under My progressions.

EXERCISES
• Tap "Felt clean" after a run to see your new best tempo count up, plus where your next session will start.

4 NEW TUNINGS
• B, Bb, A and Ab Standard for baritone, doom and down-tuned metal. That brings Fretionary to 14 tunings.

Thanks for playing with Fretionary! If it's helping your practice, a quick rating means a lot.
